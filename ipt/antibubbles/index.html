Anti-bubbles

International Physicists' Tournament 2024 · Problem 15 · École Polytechnique team

Our team's presentation for this problem at IPT 2024, where we won the silver medal (2nd of 21 countries). The problem:

Fill a container with water with decreased surface tension. Then drop a droplet of regular water into the container (very slowly). Investigate the phenomenon. Maximize the size and lifetime of an anti-bubble. Are these quantities related?
