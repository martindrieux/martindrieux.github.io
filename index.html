I work on generative models and on post-training large language models. At Carnegie Mellon I study flow maps: models that learn to jump along a generative trajectory in one or a few steps instead of hundreds. This summer at Mistral AI I worked on post-training for coding, on test writing and on the tests used to verify code.

Before CMU I studied applied math, physics and machine learning at École Polytechnique, and did research on neural PDE solvers at UC Berkeley.

Papers

  • 2026

    Equilibrium Flow Maps

    J. Lin*, M. Drieux*, J. Huang, S. Dandapanthula, N. M. Boffi · Under review, ICLR 2027

    Flow maps for time-free (autonomous) generative dynamics: one model generates in a single step or refines over many. On ImageNet 256×256 it reaches FID 2.83 in one step and 1.67 in eight, and keeps improving with more steps.
